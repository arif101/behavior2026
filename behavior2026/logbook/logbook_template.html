Failure root causes

Failures bucket by terminal EE-to-target distance. The signature is bimodal: successes press in a median of ~12 steps, while ~93% of failures time out more than 15 cm away — the policy either knows the state and commits immediately, or diverges from it entirely. Near-miss failures at the button are rare. This points at state-coverage/exploration, not precision.

Per-chunk record

One row per training chunk (a scene × rung pass), in execution order, with the rule that ended it. Budgets: stage-1 = 3,000 env steps, stage-2 = 4,500 steps or 25 episodes; early-stop fires at rolling20 ≥ 0.95 with ≥20 episodes (hit once in the whole campaign). A chunk ending with rolling20 < 0.3 has its checkpoint quarantined out of the warm-start chain. Known flaw, being replaced in the trainer rebuild: step-denominated budgets allocate episodes inversely to need (cold scenes got 10 episodes, healthy scenes up to 50).
