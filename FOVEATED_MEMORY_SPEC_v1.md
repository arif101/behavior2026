## NOVELTY VERDICT

VERDICT: ADJACENT-TO-OCCUPIED overall; the specific claimed TRIPLE is OPEN as of this sweep.

Occupied (do not claim): agent-centered multi-resolution maps (Behnke lineage since 2003, arXiv 1810.01345; re-learned by RobotPan 2604.13476); target-zoom coarse-to-fine policy inference (C2F-ARM 2106.12534, Act3D 2306.17817, RVT-2 2406.08545); voxel-feature memory with coarse-to-fine attention retrieval into a pi0.5-class mobile-manipulation VLA (EchoVLA 2511.18112, +0.20 over pi0.5 on RoboCasa; mindmap 2509.20297); 2D gaze/foveated-input manipulation policies (Kim-Kuniyoshi 2102.01295, GIAVA/Look-Focus-Act 2507.15833); prefix map tokens on pi0.5 for BEHAVIOR specifically (SERF 2606.12956, +14.7 pts task progress — but with privileged sim segmentation, GT global pose, and pre-execution feature fitting, none legal at B2026 eval).

OPEN — not found anywhere across ~5 independent sweeps, and confirmed absent from SERF/SBP full texts: (i) resolution scheduled by the wrist's PHYSICAL proximity to contact over the approach (temporal foveation on the HAND, not the base); (ii) the finest map level anchored in the WRIST frame via exact kinematic transforms so base-odometry drift structurally never enters (SERF's EE-ball tokens are EE-CENTERED READOUT of world-frame storage requiring global pose); (iii) one map feeding BOTH the perception prefix (soft tokens) and a progressively-refined metric AdaLN target — both SERF and SBP inject via prefix/concat only and never touch the motor-conditioning signal; in both, the map is frozen or degraded precisely at contact time.

Nearest 3 works and deltas: (1) EchoVLA 2511.18112 — nearest overall; delta = theirs is single-resolution world-frame declarative memory with attention-only injection, no metric target refinement, no drift story. (2) RVT-2 / Act3D / C2F-ARM family — nearest to precision-refines-toward-contact; delta = per-inference zoom on static-tabletop keyframe predictors, no persistent memory, no mobile base, no wrist anchoring. (3) GIAVA + Kim-Kuniyoshi — nearest to the foveation/gaze mechanism and independently validates our measured wrist-acquisition root cause; delta = 2D retinal foveation with gaze supervision, no 3D map, no metric injection. (SERF is the nearest SYSTEM on our benchmark but its gains are navigation/search/recovery-level — task progress plateaus ~52-64% with contact behavior inherited unchanged — i.e. it solves the half of our failure ladder that already works.)

Honest caveat on delta (ii): the wrist-frame trick is implicit in any eye-in-hand servo system; what makes it non-trivial here is the measured regime — whole-body reach moves the base during the approach (torso dims flex 87.5 deg) and the target exits the head view, so a world-frame fine map inherits 0.144 m drift >> 2 cm cells. Quantify that mismatch in the paper; it is the argument.

Additional measurement novelty: nobody A/Bs prefix-vs-AdaLN injection on a GAZE-mediated outcome — everyone reports success only. Our wrist off-axis angle + attempt-formation-rate mediating-variable methodology, plus the eval-time token-SWAP causal test, is itself a reportable contribution. Concurrent-work risk is real (EchoVLA v2 revised Mar 2026; the space is fast) — build the ablation story, not just the system, and claim narrowly: 'proximity-scheduled foveation anchored to the end-effector, with a registration-free wrist-frame fine level, injected at both the perception prefix and the metric AdaLN pathway of a flow VLA.'

## ARCHITECTURE SPEC

FOVEATED SPATIAL MEMORY v1.0 — buildable spec. Every choice below cites either a first-party measurement (FP) or a published result.

== A. MAP LEVELS (all feed-forward writes; no fitted latents, no test-time gradients — SBP's own ablation shows the static map captures ~80% of the gain and its online refit is suspended during grasping, i.e. blind exactly when we need it; mindmap/EchoVLA prove feed-forward accumulation suffices) ==

L0 COARSE (room memory): 0.5 m voxels, odometry-registered base/world frame, ~24x24x6 grid (~3.5k cells). Content: EMA-pooled backprojected SigLIP head-cam patch features (REUSE pi0.5's already-computed patch tokens — no second VFM encoder; the 24GB constraint binds on encoders, not maps) + occupancy/staleness counters. Drift tolerance: measured 0.144 m/ep << 0.5 m cells (FP). Job: persistence when target leaves all views; navigation-scale queries.

L1 MID (near-robot): 0.1 m voxels, base frame, scrolling 3x3x2 m grid (~18k cells), Behnke-style local multiresolution (cited as motivation, NOT claimed as novel — 20-year prior art, arXiv 1810.01345, RobotPan 2604.13476). Populated from head + wrist RGB-D backprojection at obs rate.

L2 FINE (the load-bearing novelty): 2 cm voxels, WRIST frame, 0.4 m cube ahead of each gripper (20^3 = 8k cells, <1 MB). Populated per-frame from that wrist's 480x480 RGB-D using only the fixed cam-to-wrist extrinsic (provided at eval). Persistence across wrist motion via FK chain transforms — exact kinematics, never odometry, so base drift NEVER enters this level by construction. Per-frame overwrite means no grasp-time blind spot (vs SBP, which suspends updates while grasping). 2 cm cells + a sub-voxel continuous offset head (features -> metric residual) give effective mm-scale precision without 8x memory — required because per-voxel grasp systems run at 7.5 mm (VGN) and our 2 cm alone is ~3x coarser; the wrist sensor supports it (~1-2 mm/px at 30-50 cm standoff).

FINE AFFORDANCE HEAD (separate small module, trained off the 11h budget): predicts, from frozen dense features on wrist RGB-D crops around the projected coarse point, an AFFORDANCE FRAME: 3D point + face normal + confidence, wrist frame. RE-INFERRED per chunk, not EMA-smoothed (RVT-2's zoom re-inference lifted insert_peg 11.2%->40%; smoothing a coarse estimate has no such evidence). Labels: OmniGibson togglebutton/grasp METALINK poses extracted via our validated replay_poses pipeline (0.5 mm, FP) — this fixes the measured centroid-vs-affordance miss at its root: ToggledOn fires on finger overlap with a ~5 cm sphere at the togglebutton metalink held 5 consecutive steps (toggle.py, first-party source read), so 2 cm accuracy SUFFICES and the centroid label was simply wrong.

== B. INJECTION SITES (dual, provably disjoint routes — FP code read: current target_state sits in the suffix with ar_mask=True so the prefix structurally CANNOT see it, and adarms_cond=[None, adarms_cond] means AdaLN never touches perception; our measured 'motor gets the point, wrists never acquire' gap is a code property, not a training failure) ==

SITE 1 — K=8 scope-structured soft tokens, appended AFTER text in embed_prefix, ar_mask=False. Appending at the end leaves every pretrained image/text RoPE position bit-identical (positions = cumsum(input_mask)-1; Gemma has no learned posembs); make_attn_mask needs zero changes; the action expert sees the tokens through the KV cache for free (pi0.py:314-336). Token layout (scope-structured per SERF's ablation — EE-scope -6.1 and robot-only -6.1 are the highest-weight scopes; environment-only is nearly free at -1.6 and is skipped):
  1 T-fine-R, 2 T-fine-L: wrist-frame affordance estimate per gripper (point+normal+conf)
  3 T-EE: robot kinematic state (FK robot points scope)
  4 T-mid: 0.1 m ball around projected target
  5 T-coarse: 0.5 m room cell around target (persistence when out of view)
  6 T-global: 4 m base-radius context
  7 T-stage: stage ID + tier ID + commit bit
  8 T-uncert: target-seen? / map staleness / confidence
Construction: small PointNet-lite/3D-conv per level + attention pooling with RELATIVE coordinates only (SERF: absolute coords omitted, anti-overfitting), MLP-reparameterized (Li & Liang: free continuous prompts are unstable), projected to 2048-d (Gemma-2B width, NOT the 1024 expert width) via a ZERO-INIT kernel, plus 0.02-scale learned register embeddings. A zero EMBEDDING is not a no-op through RMSNorm/attention — zero-init the PROJECTION so map CONTENT is exactly absent at step 0 while register tokens train; verify with a parity eval before Run 1. Step-0 behavior ~= the current 18% ckpt by construction (Flamingo/LLaMA-Adapter pattern). Recomputed once per chunk-32 (matches serving fix; +1.7% prefix FLOPs, negligible).

SITE 2 — AdaLN pathway (kept as THE motor channel: COMET's ablation on our exact task, AdaLN 77.5 vs prefix 38.5 — the prefix is the wrong sole motor route; its job here is perception/gaze). target_state slot upgraded to a format-invariant record: EE->affordance 3D vector + approach-axis normal + tier-ID one-hot + confidence + commit bit. Filled by a RESOLUTION-BY-PROXIMITY selector in the data transform (zero architecture change — it is a swap of what fills the existing slot): L0/L1 metalink prior when far, wrist-frame fine re-inference when acquired. Position alone under-specifies a press; the normal is required (GAPartNet part frames).

AUX LOSSES (all training-only, zero eval cost): (i) point-readout decode from soft-token OUTPUT states -> target pixel (head + both wrists) + 3D point, w~0.1 — kills the register/attention-sink degeneration mode (arXiv 2309.16588) AND gives the from-scratch encoder dense supervision from step 0 (we have ~100% oracle coverage); (ii) gaze-attention KL: final-VLM-layer attention (text+soft tokens -> visual patches) regularized toward the projected-target heatmap — Gaze-Regularized VLA did exactly this ON pi0 and gained 5-12% on LIBERO with measurably moved attention (arXiv 2603.23202); this is the 'want to look' gradient our failure ladder says is missing; (iii) stage-head aux on the free demo stage annotations.

== C. SWITCHING STATE MACHINE (deterministic, inference-side, mirrored exactly in scaffold training data so the policy sees consistent (stage, point-source, behavior) triples; bottleneck-pose handoff per C2F-IL 2105.06411 / DOME 2204.02863) ==

APPROACH: head/map targeting, AdaLN = metalink prior from L0/L1. ->
HANDOFF TEST (never distance alone — distance-only is the documented boundary failure and matches our 102-107 deg pathology): EE-target dist < 30 cm AND target projects inside wrist FOV with >=15% margin AND fine-head confidence > tau (calibrated to 90% precision on held-out demos) for N=3 consecutive frames (~220 ms @13.5 FPS). ->
ACQUIRE (if handoff test fails): wrist-orient macro toward the AdaLN point until visibility check passes; timeout 4 s -> fallback head-guided blind press at the coarse metalink point (the 5 cm trigger sphere gives real slack). Scripted in the scaffold engine, distilled into the policy; the LOOK motion itself is in the clips — acquisition never emerges from reach-BC (TAVIS, Vision-in-Action, DOME's explicit assumption). ->
SERVO: AdaLN = wrist-frame fine estimate, re-inferred per chunk. Downgrade to APPROACH if the fine head loses the target M=5 frames before contact. ->
COMMIT: trigger = fingertip-affordance dist < 7 cm AND approach-axis alignment < 20 deg. Freeze the fine estimate; NO re-targeting; mode-sticky chunk sampling (select the flow sample minimizing divergence from the previous chunk's terminal mode — our measured mode vacillation is a toggle-killer independent of accuracy, because any graze-and-retreat resets the 5-consecutive-step ToggledOn counter); press-and-hold through >=1 full chunk (>=5 sim steps dwell). NEVER downgrade after commit. Retry: if no toggle/contact confirmed after the commit chunk, rewind ~15 cm along the normal to standoff and re-enter SERVO (max 3; the force-open-retry scaffold generates exactly these clips in RaC rewind-then-correct format, arXiv 2509.07953).

== D. INTEGRATION (FP source-read, ~150 model-side lines) ==
Thread map_feats through Observation exactly as target_state was threaded (model.py:120/142/222 -> pi0.py embed path — the shipped template). Update fake_obs shapes (pi0_config.py:81) or lazy_init silently bakes wrong K. Fixed voxel-grid shapes for jit (no variable point counts). Map builder lives OUTSIDE the model (preproc filling an Observation field) so model code stays jit-stable. Version-gate checkpoints: write K + map shapes into ckpt metadata, assert at serve load; add the K=0 bit-identity parity test to CI before Run 1 (suffix RoPE positions shift by K valid tokens — consistent between train/eval but old-serving + new-ckpt = silent drift; the cross-fade seam bug already taught us this cost). Budget: few-M new params, +16 KV rows, >10 GB eval headroom beside sim on the 24 GB box; the only genuinely new runtime cost is the map BUILDER — profile it standalone on the eval box first (target <20 ms/frame; published TSDF integration runs ms-scale).

## AB MATRIX

DESIGN PRINCIPLE (pre-registered): primary endpoint = grasp-ATTEMPT-FORMATION RATE, mechanism endpoint = wrist off-axis angle in the final 3 s. Success rate is UNDERPOWERED and banned as primary: at n=20/arm two-sided Fisher exact detects 27%->70% formation (5/20 vs 14/20, p<0.05) but NOT 18%->40% success. At 3 rollouts/hr this is the only honest read.

## RISKS AND GATES

RANKED RISKS (failure mode -> earliest signal -> fallback):

  2. Formation improves but wrist angle doesn't (motor side-channel; R2 fail) -> signal: G1 mechanism read -> fallback: promote the deterministic ACQUIRE macro from training scaffold to PERMANENT runtime primitive (legal: deterministic state machine on legal inputs) and re-scope the paper to the AdaLN-refinement + commit contributions.
  3. Acquisition doesn't distill (BC-gap recurrence — our Phase-A verdict showed mechanical wins failing closed-loop) -> signal: Run-2 honest formation < 40% while oracle formation passes -> fallback: same as 2 (keep the macro at runtime); the literature says nobody gets acquisition free from BC, so this is a planned retreat, not a scramble.
  4. Fine affordance head fails on unseen instances (our grounding bench: 124.6 px on unseen thawing) -> signal: held-out-instance error > 2 cm at PREP gate -> fallback: tier caps at L1 + head-guided blind press at the coarse metalink prior (the ~5 cm ToggledOn sphere is the slack that makes this viable).
  5. Baseline degradation (register-token perturbation or full-FT forgetting) -> signal: step-0 parity eval delta on 5 cached obs + Arm B nav/locomotion metrics vs A -> fallback: tanh-gated variant (map features gated onto register embeddings, alpha=0) and/or SimVLA 0.1x backbone LR.

  8. Concurrent scooping (EchoVLA v2 active) -> mitigation: narrow claims + mediating-variable measurements nobody else reports; the ablation story survives even if a similar system appears.
  9. Eval-power starvation at 3 rollouts/hr -> mitigation: baked into the matrix (formation as primary, per-episode mechanism logging).

## WHAT WE DROP

Components the evidence says to CUT or DEFER, with reasons (each traced):

1. SBP-style fitted latent grids + decoder distillation: CUT. The decoder exists to serve per-scene gradient fits and latent decompression we don't need; neither paper ablates distilled latents vs directly-pooled VFM features (the comparison does not exist); SERF's env-point construction IS direct pooling before an unnecessary compression stage, and SERF's own Limitations prescribe feed-forward encoders. SBP's OOD protocol (pre-fit a latent map of the novel scene) is illegal/infeasible at B2026 eval.
2. Online gradient map refitting (SBP's 20 steps / 5 env steps): CUT. Static map captures ~80% of the gain (SBP Table: 0.82->0.94 static vs 0.97 online); updates are SUSPENDED during grasping — structurally blind at the moment we care about; our per-frame wrist-level overwrite has no such blind spot and no decoder dependency.
3. Contrastive shaping losses (SERF lambdas 0.02/0.01, SAM-2 part banks): CUT. Never ablated anywhere; exist for cross-scene semantic clustering, not geometric precision.
4. SE(3) object tracking stack (CoTracker3 + FGR + ICP): DEFER. Worth ~1.4 pts avg in SERF's own table for the largest runtime/complexity load in their system. Revisit only if moved-object tasks demand it post-G4.
5. Environment-only token scope: CUT (SERF ablation -1.6, nearly redundant with global).
6. Force/compliance sensing and control: CUT. ToggledOn requires contact-impulse PRESENCE + geometric overlap + dwell — no force magnitude, no direction (toggle.py first-party). The force-needing literature (FORGE, HIL-SERL insertions) solves tighter tolerances than a ~5 cm trigger sphere. Keep only the free gripper-effort bit as a stage-head input.
7. mm-scale voxels / super-resolution at the wrist: CUT. Sub-voxel offset head on 2 cm cells gives effective mm precision at 1/8th the memory; the binding constraint at the fine tier is VISIBILITY and LABEL SEMANTICS, never sensor resolution (480x480 @ 30 cm ~ 1-2 mm/px).
8. LoRA / frozen-VLM staged training: CUT for our runs. Our own 3-way convergent research dropped LoRA-as-protection; our AdaLN full-FT trained fine in 11 h; Prismatic shows separate projector-pretraining unnecessary. SERF's freeze+LoRA recipe is retained only as a documented fallback if forgetting appears (with SimVLA's 0.1x backbone LR as the first lever).
9. Ego3D-style depth positional encoding on wrist SigLIP features: DEFER. Genuinely cheap and orthogonal (SpatialVLA), but it perturbs the pretrained visual pathway, is not needed for any of the three claims, and violates protect-the-baseline discipline for Run 1-2. Candidate for the Sep burst if headroom exists.
10. World-frame fine map / any global 3D registration: CUT by measurement. 0.144 m/ep odometry drift >> 2 cm cells; global pose is unavailable at eval anyway. This is not a compromise — the mismatch IS the paper's argument for wrist-frame anchoring.
11. Migrating the point signal to the prefix / prefix-as-motor-pathway: CUT. COMET's ablation on our exact task (AdaLN 77.5 vs prefix 38.5) and PointVLA's design both say no. The prefix carries perception; AdaLN carries motor.
12. Interleaving new tokens between image tiles, K>16, generic (non-scope-structured) learned queries: CUT. No precedent interleaves; VPT/Q-Former/TempoVLA lineage says small K; SERF's ablation dictates the scope structure; RoPE-position preservation dictates append-after-text.
13. Success rate as the primary A/B endpoint: CUT (power arithmetic at 3 rollouts/hr; formation + wrist angle are the measurable mediators and the novelty).
14. Continuous EMA refinement of one global estimate: CUT in favor of discrete per-chunk RE-INFERENCE in the wrist frame at a bottleneck standoff (RVT-2's measured 11.2->40 zoom gain; C2F-IL/DOME phase structure) — the user's resolution-by-proximity idea is honoured as a phase-changing re-inference schedule, which is the rigorous version of it, not a smoothing filter.
