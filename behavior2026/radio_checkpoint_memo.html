What was discovered, in one paragraph

The human demos' grasps are physically fake: the collection rig pulls the object ~26 cm into the closing hand, so no recording demonstrates an earnable grasp, and the policies trained on them inherit a motion that eval's physics refuses. But the recordings are honest everywhere else — and one frame after the pull, the world is legally arranged. Restoring that frame, closing under live physics until eval's three native conditions hold for the full window, welding, and replaying the demo's own motion manufactures a physically-honest clip from every viable demo. Half the time eval's trigger fires entirely on its own.
