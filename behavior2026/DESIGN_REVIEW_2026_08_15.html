BEHAVIOR-2026 · design review · 2026-08-15 · A5000 box, 55 days to freeze

The Last Ten Centimeters

A first-principles review of the terminal-skill training pipeline and system architecture, written against measured data on this box: 49 training chunks, 848 episodes, 175,886 env steps, 40.1 h of wall clock, plus the campaign logs, the start bank, the harvest bank, and the actual BDDL goal definitions of all 100 tasks. Companion running record: the B26 Flight Log (regenerated from telemetry by logbook/build_logbook.py).

Verdict

The skill line is a success as a mode manufacturer and a dead end as a runtime architecture at scale. RL-from-restore-states is still the only method on the board that has ever created commit behavior (75% in 31 min at the easiest rung; instant episode-1 transfer across scenes), but the recipe as run is serial in scenes × rungs × tasks at 1.22 env-steps/s: finishing radio alone costs another ~100 h on this box, and the census says grasp+place — not press — gates 94 of 100 tasks.

Recommendation, in one sentence: treat the skill as a data factory, not a runtime component — rebuild the trainer for ~10× throughput (multi-scene single policy, snapshot restores, render-off, parallel envs), port the recipe to one grasp task immediately, and spend the Run-3 slot distilling skill successes back into the VLA with the FACT noise-schedule fix and a foveated-input arm, verified by the commit probe. The hand-set 0.07 m gate and the rest of the runtime handoff machinery stay only as the eval-day backstop, with the threshold replaced by a learned gate. Continuous geometric conditioning ("latent spatial intelligence") is the endpoint of that path, not a competing branch: it becomes testable exactly when the flywheel puts nonzero commit mass back into the policy.

The per-episode cost decomposes (median inter-episode deltas) into a marginal 0.51–0.63 s per env step and a fixed ~50–63 s of restore/settle overhead per episode (HDF5 playback restore, AG re-establishment, 90–150 settle steps; 477 SETTLE_RETRY events across the two passes). Extrapolation under the current recipe. Radio still needs: the rest of stage-2 (~13 chunks), stage-3 (press−100, 32 chunks at a harder rung), the boundary rung (policy-visited 10 cm-shell states, untouched — the 54-state harvest bank has never been trained on), and repeat visits to 12 cold scenes. That is ≥100 additional hours for one task, i.e. ~150 h/task naive. Three families need a credible demonstration on ~6–10 tasks before freeze: ~1,000–1,500 box-hours against ~1,300 total hours remaining — and 100 tasks would be ~15,000 h (~1.7 box-years). The recipe's structure, not its constants, is the problem: cost is O(tasks × scenes × rungs) serial, with per-family engineering (reward, success detector, gate) on top. A 10× cut in sim-steps-to-competence and wall-per-step is not an optimization — it is the difference between "radio-only demo" and "family coverage."

If the probe still reads 0.00, that is a decisive negative worth a slot: it would tell us the mode cannot enter this policy class by imitation at all, and (a)/(c) become the real architecture rather than the backstop.
(e) Input-side — affordance-centered foveated resampling replacing the uniform 720→224 downsample (button ≈ 5 px today), + terminal zoom offline BC-MSE on contact windows under resampled inputs; commit probe after retrain transform-level; rides the same Run-3 slot fold into (d) Attacks the perception side of the same mechanism the noise schedule attacks from the gradient side. Alone it is unlikely to conjure a missing action mode (BC already failed with sharp enough demos); as an arm inside the Run-3 retrain it is nearly free and externally supported (+2–2.7 pp matched-pixel-budget class evidence; fixation placement insensitive).

The synthesis

(a) is the engine, (d) is the product, (b) is the destination, (c) removes the hand-set constants in the meantime, (e) rides along in the same training slot. The deep reason this ordering is right: the runtime-gate problem largely dissolves on the factory path. In sim, the "gate" at data-collection time can be oracle-chosen restore states — no 0.07 m constant, no detector precision question, no handoff seam. Those constants only matter on the eval-day backstop path, where (c) replaces them with learned, task-relative signals.

The grasp+place gate is the binding test. The verified census (Q8): grasp+place appears in the goal of 94/100 tasks and is the whole goal of 61. Assisted-grasp physics is official eval behavior, which lowers the bar for the exact family we must win. The recipe port (reward = AG-engaged ∧ lifted; curriculum from restore states around grasp initiation; same RLPD core; picking_up_trash has the cheapest verified data path) should start within two weeks — before more radio rungs — because every architecture above is only as good as its grasp story, and today we have zero measured evidence in that family.

Q4Foveation & spatial intelligence: getting geometry into the last 10 cm

Route geometry through the small controller first. Two measured cautions argue for testing L2 on the 2M-param skill before adding more conditioning to the 3B VLA: the strongest external ablation on our own injection route found more information through AdaLN performed worse (displacement-only 68.9 vs +target+gripper 59.7), and our own G1 measured the prefix route dead. Q6Cold scenes: cheapest decisive experiment

Corrected inventory: 12 scenes with zero successes at every rung tried (d70, d110, d130, d160, d220, d240, d270, d320, d340, d350, d370, d410); d30/d50 are a different root cause — their start banks fail AG-validity at restore (RESET_EXHAUSTED, ep=0), a restore-fidelity defect, not a learning failure. The referenced posture-cluster AUC 0.77 analysis does not exist on this box and should be treated as unverified.

What we already know: warm-starting converts at least some cold scenes — d270 went 0/10 fresh at stage 1, and is producing successes right now at stage 2 under d170's checkpoint (first at EP 5). And the step-cap starvation note in train_skill.py is real: cold chunks got ~10 episodes while healthy ones got 30–55, so cold scenes have also simply seen less training.

The experiment (fits in ~1 box-day at current throughput, less after the Q2 rebuild): on 4 hard-cold scenes (d110, d130, d160, d220), a 2×2 of {fresh vs best-warm-start} × {blind vs L2-on}, 15 episodes per cell, plus demo-replay seeding (replay the demo's own actions from the restore frame to plant 2–3 successes in the buffer) as a fifth arm on two of them. Offline in parallel: the orientation analysis the brief wanted — EE-approach yaw/side relative to the radio at bank states, cold vs warm, reported with honest AUC. Does the answer change architecture? Mostly no: warm-start and seeding are curriculum/bootstrap fixes, and cold scenes only shrink the data factory's coverage. The one branch that is architectural: if L2-on is what converts them, the un-blinding becomes load-bearing and belongs in the skill's contract (and, via the flywheel, in the VLA's inputs).

Evaluation methodology (standing rules, tightened)

  • Instance 301 stays untouchable (never train/restore/harvest), and bars freeze at pre-registration before data arrives — unchanged; it is why this project's numbers are trustworthy.
  • Power before verdicts: n=25 arms resolve only large effects (±16 pp at p≈0.2); no kill decisions off n=5 arms; one-sided pre-registered tests; n≥40 for gate decisions where the budget allows.
  • No arm-C before the detector clears ≥0.9 precision against sim ground truth (48 h item 2).
  • Every eval episode writes a flight-recorder JSON with the fixed failure taxonomy (nav / approach / gate-never-fired / skill-window-fail / detector-miss / success-then-lost), and the Flight Log regenerates from telemetry — success rates and root causes live in one place, dated, with the raw sources named.
  • Single-variable arms; every claim traces to a measurement — unchanged. Numbers read off figures are recorded as such (08-09 lesson).

ADDENDUMThe primitive-library plan of record (added 2026-08-16)

Adopted after design discussion. Thesis: the VLA is a latent action bank either way — flow-BC on demos writes entries keyed by visual context (memorization; the commit probe's collapsed diversity, p10–p90 spread 0.004 rad, is what a lookup table looks like from outside), while the approach behavior proves the alternative already lives in the same network: it is keyed by a geometric input (displacement → AdaLN) and warps correctly to unseen layouts. The upgrade is therefore not a new module but control over two things: what the keys are, and how the missing entries get manufactured. Keys = affordance frame + L2 wrist-frame geometry + contact/in-grasp classification at terminal (resolution-by-proximity, AdaLN route). Entries = RL-manufactured commit/grasp data via the flywheel, trained under the FACT logit-normal schedule with foveated inputs and noise-randomized keys.
