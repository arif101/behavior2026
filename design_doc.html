1 · Why 26% is the ceiling today

The failure model that drives every design choice below.

Episodes run ~20 minutes. A behavior-cloned policy that is 99.9% right per step still compounds to near-zero over that horizon — one unrecovered failure kills the episode. The 2025 winner's answer was hand-coded correction tables; NVIDIA's was post-hoc RFT. The gap between deployed and achievable is documented:

2025 winner (deployed)
0.260
NVIDIA + RFT (post-hoc)
0.345
best-checkpoint oracle
0.611

The 0.26 → 0.61 gap is a no-recovery, no-self-knowledge gap — not a backbone-capacity gap. That is where our contribution budget goes.

  • Memorized conditioning: the 2025 winner replaces language with task-ID embeddings — no perception in the conditioning path, zero transfer to the 50 new tasks. Hidden-instance ranking punishes exactly this.
  • Metric mechanics: q pays partial credit on the final state only (achieved-then-lost = 0), and hidden-set rank tracks breadth (2025 nonzero-q task counts: 43 / 42 / 15 for ranks 1–3).

2 · Architecture — four additions to pi05_b1k

Ranked by expected Δq. Click any axis for the mechanism and evidence.

Success-replay RFT + corrective-DAgger from failure states using privileged scripted teachers (sim state is legal at train time) + AWR through the loader — advantage = predicate-delta, applied as sampling weights, no off-manifold perturbation.

Doctrine (hard-won on LIBERO; independently validated by Disney's ReActor at SIGGRAPH 26): physics-executed teacher data only. No kinematic conversion, no dwell states, no sub-resolution decision features, no saturated control regimes.

Optional: frozen motion-prior as a rollout scorer (SMP recipe, SIGGRAPH 26) — prototype only if cheap.

Replace task-ID-style conditioning with depth-grounded relative 3D target points injected into the action expert (AdaLN or dedicated token), re-grounded per stage.

Train-time labels come free from privileged sim poses; at inference the point comes from depth + a learned grounding head — our LIBERO grounding lineage. Mechanism externally validated at +32–46pp on π0.5/GR00T with oracle grounding (arXiv 2606.27663) — they punted on honest grounding; we own it.

A second-order head predicts P(failure | stage), conformally calibrated on our own eval-fleet rollouts. On trigger, dispatch a trained recovery behavior — retract-reapproach, re-grasp, re-ground — not a hand-coded rule table.

This is our prediction-error / ε research line made concrete.

Stage-prediction aux head with voting (adopted from Larchenko, cited) anchors long-horizon progress. A director layer reads the BDDL goal (known at eval): sequences flippable literals, picks the cheapest goal option, retries on no-progress, and parks the end state — the metric scores the final state only. Plus the anti-fall embodiment YAML.

Adopted techniques (cited, orthogonal to memorization): correlated-noise flow matching · multi-sample FM (15/step) · delta actions + per-timestep normalization · soft inpainting 30/26/4 · receding horizon (temporal ensembling scored 0.00 in 2025) · absolute joints · 448² head-camera A/B gated on the 24 GB inference budget.

4 · Data plan

  • The public dataset is one monolithic LeRobot-v3 repo (20k eps / 100 tasks / 210.9M frames) — the docs' per-task download filter matches nothing. Solved twice over: surgical file download via the episode→chunk mapping (radio task = 200 eps in 12 files / 2.1 GB), and episode_filter at load time.
  • v1 task set: the fat-middle pick-place cluster (79% of all goal literals are kinematic placement) + single-literal quick wins. Abandon the particle/cleaning dead tail — zero for every team in 2025.
  • Norm stats, FAST tokenizer, and the Cholesky factor (for correlated noise) recomputed per task-set on 2026 data.

6 · Risks — honest

Training computeFull π0.5 fine-tune wants H100-class; the 5090 (32 GB) likely runs LoRA/low-mem only, so G2 may run at reduced fidelity. Budget the H100 burst early.
Grounding at 720² household scaleHarder than LIBERO tabletop. Mitigation: privileged-label pretraining + depth (RGBD is given at eval).
No warm-start = no free carryover qOur first leaderboard number lands ~2–3 weeks later than a warm-started banker would. Accepted trade for ownership.
Sim throughputFull self-eval ≈ 350–450 GPU-h. The jobqueue eval fleet is a first-class deliverable, not an afterthought.
Metric mechanics disciplineEnd-state parking and cheapest-option selection are free points — enforce in the director from day one.
