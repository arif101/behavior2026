2026-08-20 telemetry morning: reward desert + grip jitter found and fixed (v21h, v21i)

Two rapid telemetry-driven iterations after the assist curriculum went live. v21h added grasp-family shaping (one-time +0.3 on AG acquisition; potential-based lift progress paying only NEW height) after reading the reward code: the press-era terms end at finger contact, leaving a sparse desert across the entire acquire→lift→hold segment.
